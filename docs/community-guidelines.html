Community Guidelines

Last Updated: August 2026

1 Be Respectful, Even Anonymously

Loona is built on anonymity to let you express yourself freely, not to give you a mask for cruelty. Do not engage in targeted harassment, bullying, or hate speech against any individual, group, or college faculty.

2 No NSFW or Explicit Content

Our platform is for campus life, not explicit material. Do not post nudity, sexually explicit content, or highly graphic imagery. Violations will result in immediate device bans.

3 Protect Personal Information

Do not dox yourself or others. Do not post phone numbers, private emails, home addresses, or highly sensitive personal secrets that could identify a specific student. Our automated systems may block posts containing potential phone numbers or emails.

4 Keep the Campus Shop Honest

When using the Campus Shop or Bargains hub, ensure your listings are legitimate. Do not scam other students, post illegal items, or sell prohibited substances.

5 Reporting & Enforcement

We rely on the community to self-regulate. If a post violates these guidelines, use the Report button. Posts flagged multiple times are temporarily hidden and sent for manual review by our moderation team. We reserve the right to permanently ban users who repeatedly violate these rules.
