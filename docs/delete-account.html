Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Loona account and all associated data. This includes your posts, confessions, messages, and profile information.

⏱️ 30-Day Grace Period — You can cancel anytime within 30 days

📦 What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name, avatar, and bio
  • All posts, confessions, and comments you've made
  • All chat messages and conversations
  • Your potato (karma) points and badges
  • All saved posts and notification history

✅ 30-Day Grace Period

Your account is not deleted immediately. It enters a 30-day grace period.

During this time, you can log back into the app to cancel the deletion and restore your account fully.

After 30 days, all data is permanently and irrevocably deleted. This cannot be undone.

🎯 How to delete from the app (easiest)

1

Open the Loona app on your phone

2

Tap the Profile tab (bottom right)

3

Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top right

4

Scroll down to Support → Delete Account

5

Tap Schedule Deletion and confirm. You'll be logged out and your 30-day grace period begins.
