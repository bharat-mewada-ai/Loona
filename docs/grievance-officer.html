Complaint Mechanism

When filing a grievance, please ensure you provide the following information to help us resolve the issue swiftly:

  • Your full name and contact details (if not preferring to remain anonymous).
  • A clear description of the issue or the exact location/ID of the post in question.
  • Any supporting evidence, such as screenshots.

We are committed to acknowledging your complaint within 24 hours and resolving it within 15 days from the date of receipt, as mandated by law.
